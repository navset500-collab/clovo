# Clovo — demo and submission notes

## 75-second demo

1. **Problem (10 seconds):** “I can build an MVP, but I don’t know whether anyone wants it. Clovo helps me ask better questions before I build more.”
2. **Input (10 seconds):** Open Test your idea. Show the written idea, audience, PDF/video options and optional prototype link. A file is not required.
3. **Agents (15 seconds):** Run a review. Open Juno in the agent map: Mira reads the material, Sora checks demand evidence, three Juno sub-agents simulate different perspectives, Rook challenges assumptions, Leo prioritizes changes, and Vera designs a real test.
4. **Results (15 seconds):** Show Test panel and Build next. Explain that the personas are simulations and cannot validate real demand. Show a concrete improvement and its acceptance check.
5. **Real people (15 seconds):** Open Real feedback, preview and publish a feedback link, then open the participant form. Real submissions populate their own counts and feedback cards. You invite participants; Clovo does not claim to have a recruited tester network.
6. **Close (10 seconds):** “We elevate the ordinary act of asking ‘is this a good idea?’ into a repeatable process: inspect, question, improve, and test with people.”

## Submission points

- Inspiration: the uncertainty of building a student project before knowing what people need.
- What it does: turns ideas or MVP material into an evidence-aware review, varied simulated reactions, prioritized changes, and a no-login human-feedback study.
- Theme: elevates everyday ideation and informal feedback into a structured learning loop.
- Technical work: Next.js/React/TypeScript, streaming multi-agent orchestration, Gemini multimodal PDF/image review, client video-frame extraction, private Vercel Blob study storage, capability-based owner access, bilingual UI, and schema validation.
- Honest limitations: AI simulations are not people; video audio is not processed; live prototype URLs are for human testing; Google Search grounding is unavailable under the current quota, so current demand is explicitly unverified.
- Built with assistance: disclose AI-assisted development and Gemini use according to the event’s submission requirements. Do not claim six independently trained models, real user recruitment, proven product-market fit, or measured commercial success.
- UI reference: Sombra inspired the visible agent workflow. Clovo has its own implementation and original logo/diagrams. Fonts are Zodiak and General Sans from Fontshare.

A good live demo is specific: explain one real uncertainty, show one useful disagreement, and invite one real person to respond. Avoid presenting the illustrative example or disposable QA responses as research results.
